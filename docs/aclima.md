# What is Aclima?

https://aclima.earth/

Aclima was founded about 10 years ago. It is a small company. It's main product is "hyperlocal" air quality measurement. This means that air quality is measured via mobile platforms (cars) that drive around a measurement area and record ambient concentrations. This is then sent through cell signal from the vehicles and is combined and analyzed into a road segment field of data. The proudct is the calibrated measurements as raw data and derived indicators. There used to be a flagship UI called Aclima Pro but it was deprecated years ago.

# Clients
Traditionally, mostly regulatory organization. Biggest client was CARB for the 2025-2026 California SMMI Campaign in 62 Underserved communities in.

# Method
A fleet of electric or hybrid cars drive around a designated campaign area. The measurements must be repeated many times, usually more than 25 measurements for a single road segment. A segment is about 200 meters. The data is collected with modality (sensor type) and GPS coordinates. This is then sent back to our cloud data analysis pipeline. There is only a few minutes between measurement and data arriving in our pipeline. The data is then reviewed by the data operations team. Calibration is complete before, during, and after campaigns.

## Modalities
The measured ambient concentrations are NO2, Particulate Matter, Black Carbon, Ozone, CO, CO2, Methane.

## Derived Products
Methane leaks, Diesel Combustion, Non-diesel combustion, PM2.5

# The Pivot
Federal money has dried up for general air quality measurement. Without any contract, an emergency pivot toward datacenters is being attempted. Aclima is attempting to jump in between the triad of Datacenters, Communities, and Regulators in contested land, and be the arbitrator of truth.

## Communitites
We act as a community concern gathering and analysis tool. This allows the community to report their concerns, self-curate.

## Regulators
We provide centralized visbility to combined regulatory stationary sensors and our mobile monitoring. We provide an extension to regulators sensing abilities, way beyond a few stationary sensors. This lets them make better enforcement decisions.

## Industry
We allow industry to operate at their maximum "community and regulatory safe" output. This unblocks them from blocks to building or operating.
